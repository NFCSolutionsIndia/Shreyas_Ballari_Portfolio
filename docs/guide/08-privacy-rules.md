# 8. Privacy rules

Shreyas is a minor, and this repository and the website are **public**: anyone in the world can
read every file, including old versions in the history. These rules protect him.

## Never put these anywhere in the project

Not in code, content, pictures, scans, PDFs, file names, commit messages or docs:

- Email address
- Phone number
- Home address, home town or ZIP code
- Birth date
- Daily or weekly schedule (where he is and when)

What the site may show: his name, his school (Fulton Science Academy), the school's town
(Alpharetta, Georgia), the state, and his work.

## Honest content

- **Never invent facts.** Awards, scores, courses and dates come from the family. Anything not yet
  supplied uses `placeholder(...)` (see [Change the text](05-change-text.md)).
- **Oncure** is always described as a conceptual research proposal, not an existing or clinically
  validated treatment.
- **The trading app** shows sample data, and says it is a hypothetical concept, not financial advice.
- **Illustrations** that are not real photos of him or his work are labelled as illustrations.

## Visitor privacy

- The camera (hand gestures) and microphone (voice) never start on their own. The visitor must
  click to agree, and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
- The spoken guide is muted until the visitor turns it on.
- No secret keys (API keys, passwords) ever go in the code. Anything in the site's code can be
  read by every visitor.

## If something private was pushed by mistake

Deleting the file in a new commit is **not enough**: it stays in the history. Tell a parent right
away; the history needs to be cleaned and the information treated as public.
